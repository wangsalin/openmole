# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want OpenMole to be a practical and respectful open-source project for builders working on AI SaaS infrastructure.

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, focus on technical substance, and treat others with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent when reviewing code or issues.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing clear reproduction steps and context.
- Disclose security issues privately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, low-effort promotional content, or unrelated discussions.
- Public disclosure of vulnerabilities before maintainers have had time to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.
